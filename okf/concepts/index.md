# Concepts

Grounded in the [architecture decisions](https://github.com/gellsmore-svg/mahalath/blob/main/docs/architecture-decisions.md)
and [`docs/terminology.md`](https://github.com/gellsmore-svg/mahalath/blob/main/docs/terminology.md).

- **[Lexicon of meanings](lexicon-of-meanings.md)** — opaque labels for meanings,
  not words.
- **[Frames & polysemy](frames-and-polysemy.md)** — one term, several co-equal
  frame-keyed meanings.
- **[Debate & consensus](debate-and-consensus.md)** — PrecisionCritic +
  SynthesisExplorer iterate to a consensus definition.
- **[Hierarchy](hierarchy.md)** — parent/child placement by multi-pass consensus.
- **[Provenance & staleness](provenance-and-staleness.md)** — references, reverse
  index, and self-healing when upstream meanings change.
- **[Operator review](operator-review.md)** — proposal queues a human approves.
- **[Languages & mappings](languages-and-mappings.md)** — per-language lexicons;
  cross-language mappings are explicit, debated assertions.
- **[Local-first & frontier](local-first-and-frontier.md)** — Ollama by default,
  Claude for low-confidence review.
